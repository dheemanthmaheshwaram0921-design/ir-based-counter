Diode Circuit Architecture

1. IR Emitter Diode (LED): Continuously emits invisible 940nm infrared light beams across doorway.

2. Photodiode Receiver: Operates in reverse bias mode. Beam interruption alters reverse current flow to signal passage.

3. IN4007 Protection Diode: Prevents reverse polarity damage to microcontroller inputs during voltage spikes.
